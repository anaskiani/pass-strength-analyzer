About Us

SecurityGuard is an advanced academic project designed to demonstrate the critical importance of password security through real-time analysis and cryptographic principles.

🎯 Our Mission

To educate users on cybersecurity best practices by providing instant, transparent feedback on password strength and demonstrating how encryption works under the hood.

🛡️ formatting Technology

Built with Flask, Vanilla JS, and the zxcvbn library. We utilize client-side AES encryption and server-side SHA-256 hashing to ensure a robust academic demonstration.

The Project Team

Developed as a comprehensive study in web security and full-stack development.
